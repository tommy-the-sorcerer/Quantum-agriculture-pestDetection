Instead of feeding raw numbers into a classical computer, FALCON-Q treats each agricultural measurement like a rotation dial on a 3D quantum sphere. Below is how our 3 core equations transform real farm data into an outbreak prediction in plain, everyday language.

Step 1: Converting Farm Data into Quantum Angles (ZZFeatureMap)

Data Encoding
|ψ(x)⟩ = exp[ i ∑ x_j Z_j + i ∑ (π - x_j)(π - x_k) Z_j Z_k ] · H^⊗4 |0000⟩
In Plain Everyday Words:
• H^⊗4 (Hadamard Superposition): Starts all 4 qubits spinning like coins on a table. This unlocks all 16 possible combinations (2^4 = 16) at the exact same moment.
• x_j Z_j (Single Feature Rotation): Turns each qubit by our 4 farm values: Q0 = leaf browning, Q1 = leaf greenness, Q2 = heat stress, Q3 = air moisture.
• (π - x_j)(π - x_k) Z_j Z_k (Quantum Entanglement): Links pairs of qubits together. It acts like an intelligent multiplier: "If humidity is 90% AND foliar greenness is dropping, entangle both signals to trigger an early warning alert!" Classical AI struggles to compute these non-linear couplings without massive data tables.
